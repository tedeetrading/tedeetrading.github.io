Privacy policy

YouTube Automatisering is a private automation tool operated by TD Trading (sole proprietorship, the Netherlands). Last updated: 16 November 2026.

Who uses this app

The app is used exclusively by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to other people.

What Google data the app accesses

  • Google Drive (only files created by the app): to store the owner's own music and video files in the owner's own Drive.
  • YouTube: to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and manage their publication status.

How data is used and shared

Data is used only to perform the actions above for the owner. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Access tokens are stored encrypted as secrets in the owner's private infrastructure.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
